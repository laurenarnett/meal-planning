Meal Planner SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Meal Planner SMS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you sign up to receive Meal Planner SMS notifications.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide during signup, including your name and mobile phone number.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Send weekly meal planning text messages.
  • Send grocery list notifications generated from your meal selections.
  • Manage your SMS subscription.

Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers collected through the Meal Planner SMS signup process are used solely to provide the requested SMS service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Messaging

Message frequency is approximately one message per week.

Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

For assistance, reply HELP.
